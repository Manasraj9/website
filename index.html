Featured Product — CfyBLE0

The CfyBLE0 is a compact Bluetooth Low Energy module built for smart electronics and IoT applications.

It supports multiple communication and control interfaces for flexible product integration, making it the perfect brain for your next smart device.

Designed for:

  • Smart LED lights
  • Smart home devices
  • Wireless embedded systems
  • Low-power IoT products

Key Integrations

  • 32-bit low-power MCU
  • BLE 5.2 support
  • PWM, UART, SPI, I2C, ADC interfaces
  • Up to +10 dBm RF output
  • Compact PCB antenna design
